The Goal

Every Dollar Counts

This year’s gala aims to raise $100,000 for scholarships — but the night is bigger than one evening. It marks the start of our ultimate mission: raising $1,000,000 to expand access, ease financial barriers, and empower the next generation of student leaders to Achieve M.O.O.R.E. Every ticket purchased and every donation moves the mission forward.
